# Phase 1 Kickoff — Sales CRM Platform

Subject: Phase 1 Kickoff - Sales CRM Platform

## Project overview
Phase 1 covers the core lead-to-deal lifecycle: Login/RBAC, Lead
Management, Account Management, Contact Management, and Deal/
Opportunity Management, along with the supporting Dashboard,
Notifications, and Activity tracking.

## Scope updates from the review meeting
Build against these, not the earlier version of the Scope Doc/Figma:

- Deal stages: Received Requirements, Qualified to Buy, Evaluation,
  Proposals, Contracts, Closed Won, Closed Lost, Cold Deals
- Lead Status field removed — Tier field is sufficient for
  filtering/prioritization
- LinkedIn URL field added to Lead details
- Task/activity fields added to Leads for reminders and follow-up
  tracking
- Pre-Sales Checklist will be minimal and static for Phase 1 (not
  dynamic)
- Document Management, Staff Augmentation, and dynamic checklist
  remain out of scope for Phase 1
